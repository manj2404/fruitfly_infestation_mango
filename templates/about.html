Project Title

Temperature-Based Phenology Model for Oriental Fruit Fly Detection in Mango

Objective

Help mango farmers spot fruit fly infestation early and know the pest's likely life stage, so control measures land at the right time.

Methodology

A model checks the image is a mango, then flags it healthy or infected. Daily max/min temperatures feed a Growing Degree Days (GDD) calculation that estimates the pest's stage and drives the risk level shown.

GDD Formula

GDD = ((T_max + T_min) / 2) − Base Temperature (10°C)
