Project Objective

Oriental fruit fly (Bactrocera dorsalis) infestation is one of the most damaging threats to mango cultivation. This system combines a trained image-classification model with a temperature-driven Growing Degree Days calculation to flag infected fruit and estimate which life stage the pest has likely reached — egg, larva, pupa, or adult.

Detection Flow

  1. 01 Upload mango image
  2. 02 Mango verification
  3. 03 Fruit fly detection
  4. 04 Temperature input
  5. 05 GDD calculation
  6. 06 Risk analysis
  7. 07 Recommendation & report
